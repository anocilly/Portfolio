I began my test engineering internship at SRCTec by shadowing engineers through their daily tasks.
My first task after growing accustomed to the workplace was to calibrate radio frequency (RF) devices and work through voltage, resitivity, and continuity tests for circuit boards of a radar product line.
I then was assigned my two main tasks for the summer: contributing to the deployment of WATS, a data management and analysis platform, and organization and maintenance of a lab storage room.
With WATS, I set up several data display dashboards for various product lines and conducted test step analyses of another radar product line's voltage receive tests, documenting my work for future use.
I also wrote several APIs with one of my co-interns that would link WATS to MaintainX in order to automate creation of problem reports for test failures.
Aside, I also troubleshooted a dysfunctional fan setup in one radar system and spliced wiring for a bench test setup.
